# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds this project:

- Reviewing the work, not the person. Engineering disagreement is expected and welcome.
- Giving reasons. "This fails under X" beats "this is wrong".
- Accepting correction gracefully, and correcting others without contempt.
- Assuming competence and good faith in contributors whose context you do not have.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or dismissing contributors rather than their arguments.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and
review comments — and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers via a private GitHub message or by opening an
issue titled `conduct: request contact` with no detail. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers will respond proportionately: a private correction, a public correction, a temporary
ban from interaction, or a permanent ban for repeated or severe violations. Maintainers who do not
follow or enforce this code in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions determined
by other maintainers.
